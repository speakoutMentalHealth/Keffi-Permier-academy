Mental health first

Premier Wellness Club

A school-wide student wellbeing framework that coordinates existing clubs while using positive engagement, leadership, creativity and technology to support healthy development.

Primary goal: Student mental health and overall wellbeing remain the central purpose of the programme. Clubs are structured pathways for belonging, confidence, resilience, leadership and healthy peer engagement.

ICT Club

Digital confidence, problem solving and responsible technology use.

STEM Club

Curiosity, experimentation, teamwork and innovation.

Spelling Bee & Literacy

Communication, reading culture, confidence and expression.

Cultural & Drama Club

Identity, creativity, performance, expression and teamwork.

Muslim Students Club

Structured student engagement within the School's approved framework and safeguarding standards.

Leadership Development

Student leadership, mentoring, service and personal development.

Trained Volunteers
