Premier Wellness Club.

A unified framework for student mental health, belonging, leadership, clubs, family engagement and digital support.

Primary objective

Mental health and overall student wellbeing remain the central purpose.

Clubs become pathways for belonging, confidence, expression and support.

ICT, STEM, Literacy, Culture & Drama, Muslim Students and Leadership operate under the Premier Wellness Club framework, with trained volunteers supporting each participating club.

Trained Volunteers

At least one trained volunteer/facilitator supports each participating club.

Parents & Teachers

Workshops and resources strengthen the support system around students.

Train-the-Trainer

School personnel progressively build capacity for sustainable delivery.
