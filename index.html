The KPA Difference

Education should do more than prepare a child for the next examination.

It should prepare them to think clearly, act with confidence, care for themselves and others, and step into the future ready.

Keffi Premier Academy brings academics, technology, creativity, leadership and emotional wellbeing together in one intentional student experience.
