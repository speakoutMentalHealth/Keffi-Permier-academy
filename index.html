Premier Wellness Club

Student wellbeing is the foundation.

The Premier Wellness Club coordinates existing student clubs under one structured wellbeing framework while preserving each club's unique identity.

✓
Mental health and emotional wellbeing
The central purpose across activities and support.
✓
Trained club volunteers
At least one trained facilitator supporting each participating club.
✓
Parent and teacher capacity building
Workshops, resources and Train-the-Trainer support.
✓
Paperless reporting
Digital access through personalized student and parent portal services.
